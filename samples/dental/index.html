Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so you don't wait. For tooth pain, call or message and we'll fit you in the same day where possible.

Is a root canal painful?

It is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. Mild soreness for a day or two afterwards is normal.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards and cash are accepted at the clinic.

Is there parking?

Two-wheeler parking in front and car parking in the building basement.
